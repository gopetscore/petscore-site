Method

One score, four parts. Nothing hidden.

Every food is scored on the same additive scale. Supplements use a dedicated axis — rated on active ingredients & label cleanliness, not as a food — so a fish oil isn’t punished for not being a meal.

35

Protein source quality — 35 pts

Named whole proteins first score highest. Meals, by-products and vague “meat” terms score lower, weighted by where they sit in the ingredient list.

25

Nutritional balance — 25 pts

Protein / fat / fibre balance for the product’s category — kibble, wet food, treats and toppers are each judged against their own job.

25

Clean label — 25 pts

Each concern is listed on its own line with a plain-language note on why it matters, and a red / orange / yellow severity dot.

15

Helpful extras — 15 pts

Specific, named benefits — e.g. “Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) — skin, coat & joint support”, probiotics, glucosamine — each credited individually.

What PetScore is — and isn’t

PetScore helps you read the label: what’s actually in the bag, in what order, and what’s just sprinkled on for the front of the pack. A joint treat that lists “Hip & Joint” on the front but contains no glucosamine or chondroitin will say so.

It does not replace a veterinarian, and it doesn’t rank brands by who pays the most. If a product isn’t in the database yet, the extension scores it live from the page’s ingredient list — and invites the shopper to help add it.

Privacy Policy · Last updated Oct 30, 2026

Privacy Policy — PetScore

Operator: Vicky Hao Group LLC (“PetScore”, “we”). This policy covers the PetScore browser extension and this page. It is written for users — and it is the same text submitted to the Chrome Web Store and to affiliate program reviewers.

What the extension does

When you open a supported pet product page, the extension reads the product title and ingredient text on that page, in your browser, matches it against the PetScore scoring database, and displays a score. Scoring of unlisted products happens from the page text in the same way.

What we collect

  • No account, no name, no email is required to use PetScore, and none is collected by the extension.
  • No browsing history is collected or transmitted. The extension activates only on supported pet product pages.
  • Product suggestions: if you tap “Help us add this product to our database”, the product name / page identifier you submitted is sent so we can research and add it. Nothing else is attached.
  • Affiliate clicks: if you click a retailer link from the score panel, the retailer and its affiliate network (Impact) may set cookies to attribute the order, under their own privacy policies. That is how PetScore is funded.

What we never do

  • We never sell personal data. We have none to sell.
  • We never change a score because of a commission, a brand payment, or an affiliate relationship.
  • We never place an affiliate tag on Amazon from the extension or any future app, per Amazon’s terms.

Affiliate disclosure

PetScore is free. When you buy through a tracked retailer link from the score panel, the retailer may pay us a commission at no additional cost to you. This disclosure also appears inside the extension, at the bottom of the score panel. Scores are calculated from labels only and are not veterinary advice; always consult your veterinarian for medical or dietary decisions for your pet.

Children, changes & contact

PetScore is a shopping tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13. If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact method listed on the PetScore Chrome Web Store listing.
